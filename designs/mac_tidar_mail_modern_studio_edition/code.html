Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Mac Tidar Mail and our suite ecosystem.

What is Mac Tidar Mail?
Mac Tidar Mail is an ultra-fast, human-centric webmail application built for professionals, founders, and modern teams. It features zero-loss offline drafting, sub-40ms vector search, and unified workspace organization.
How do I create an account?
Click on the 'Get Started' or 'Create Your Account' buttons on this page. You will be guided through a simple registration process to reserve your personalized address.
Will my email address end in @mactidar.com?
Yes! All native accounts registered through the service receive an authentic yourname@mactidar.com email address. Custom domains are also supported for enterprise teams.
Can I use Mac Tidar Mail on my phone?
Yes. Mac Tidar Mail is a responsive Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it on iOS Safari or Android Chrome with offline cache and instant push notifications.
Is Mac Tidar Mail part of Mac Tidar Cloud?
Yes, Mac Tidar Mail is closely integrated with Mac Tidar Cloud for document collaboration, encrypted attachment hosting, and team permissions.
Can I access other Mac Tidar products with the same account?
Absolutely. Your Mac Tidar credentials work as a Single Sign-On (SSO) passkey across the entire Mac Tidar Suite.
