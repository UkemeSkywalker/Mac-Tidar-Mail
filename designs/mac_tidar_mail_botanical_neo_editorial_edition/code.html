Inquiries §05

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Mac Tidar Mail and our suite ecosystem.

What is Mac Tidar Mail?
Mac Tidar Mail is an ultra-fast, human-centric webmail application built for professionals, founders, and modern teams. It features zero-loss offline drafting via CRDTs, sub-40ms pgvector search, and unified workspace organization.
How do I create an account?
Click on the 'Get Started' or 'Create Your Account' buttons on this page. You will be guided through a simple registration terminal to reserve your personalized address.
Will my email address end in @mactidar.com?
Yes! All native accounts registered through the service receive an authentic yourname@mactidar.com email address. Custom domains are also supported for enterprise teams.
Can I use Mac Tidar Mail on my phone?
Yes. Mac Tidar Mail is built as an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it on iOS Safari or Android Chrome with offline cache, instant push notifications, and biometric sign-in.
Is Mac Tidar Mail part of Mac Tidar Cloud?
Yes, Mac Tidar Mail is integrated with Mac Tidar Cloud for rapid document collaboration, encrypted attachment hosting, and shared organization permissions.
Can I access other Mac Tidar products with the same account?
Absolutely. Your Mac Tidar credentials work as a unified Single Sign-On (SSO) passkey across the entire Mac Tidar Suite.
