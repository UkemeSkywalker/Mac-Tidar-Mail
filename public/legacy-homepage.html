Questions, answered

Good to know.

Clear answers for the current Mac Tidar Mail prototype.

What is Mac Tidar Mail?

Mac Tidar Mail is a modern email product concept from the Mac Tidar Suite, designed to make everyday communication feel clearer and more considered. The current experience is an interactive demo.

How do I create an account?

In this prototype, Get Started opens a locally simulated account experience. It does not create a production account or send a real email.

Will my email address end in @mactidar.com?

That is the intended email domain for Mac Tidar Mail. The current demo uses example @mactidar.com addresses only.

Can I use Mac Tidar Mail on my phone?

Yes. The demo interface is responsive and adapts for smaller screens. Native mobile apps are not part of the current prototype.

Is Mac Tidar Mail part of Mac Tidar Cloud?

Mac Tidar Mail is part of the broader Mac Tidar Suite vision. Mac Tidar Cloud and connected account experiences are planned concepts and are not live in this demo.

Is Mac Tidar Mail free?

Pricing has not been announced. This prototype does not process payments or provide a production email service.
